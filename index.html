Waypoint Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Short version: Waypoint has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Everything you log stays on your phone. The only thing that ever leaves it is a food photo (and any note you type with it) when you choose to analyse a meal online with Google Gemini, using your own Gemini API key. If you use the on-device option instead, meal photos are analysed on your phone and are not sent anywhere.

Who we are

Waypoint is an Android app for tracking weight, food, water and steps, published by N Infinity. Questions about this policy: nityasingh030301@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only in Waypoint's private storage on your device:

  • Weigh-ins, body measurements, notes and progress photos you add
  • Meals (names, calories, protein, carbs, fat, optional photos), water you log, and your goals
  • Your profile (height, sex, year of birth, units) and app settings

We (the developer) never receive any of it. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Waypoint, so this data is not copied to your Google account either.

Steps from Health Connect

If you allow it, Waypoint reads your daily step count from Health Connect to show it on the Today screen. It only reads steps (no other health data) and never writes to Health Connect. Step counts are read when the screen is shown and are not saved by Waypoint or sent anywhere. You can revoke access at any time in Health Connect.

Waypoint's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements: the data is used only to show you your steps, is never used for advertising, is never sold, and is never transferred to anyone.

Food photos analysed on your phone (optional)

If you choose "On this phone", Waypoint downloads Google's open Gemma model (about 2.6 GB) once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), and then analyses meal photos and notes entirely on your device. As with any download, Hugging Face receives a standard web request (such as your IP address) when the model is fetched; no photos, notes or other data are sent. You can remove the model at any time in the app.

Food photos and Google Gemini (optional)

Meal photo analysis is optional and only works after you add your own Gemini API key from Google. When you take or pick a photo and tap to analyse it, Waypoint sends that photo (resized, as a JPEG) and any note you typed directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API, which estimates the food and its calories. Google may use Google Search to help with the estimate. This request goes from your phone to Google; it does not pass through us. Google handles it under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Depending on your Gemini plan, Google may keep or use requests to improve its services; on the free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products. See those terms for details.

Your API key is stored only in Waypoint's private storage on your device. It is not included in backups or exports.

Backups and exports

Waypoint creates backup or CSV files only when you ask it to, and hands them to Android's share sheet. Where they go from there (for example a cloud drive or email) is your choice and is governed by that service.

Notifications

Weigh-in reminders are scheduled on your device. No data is sent to send them.

Permissions

  • Internet: only for the optional online Gemini meal analysis and the one-time download of the on-device model.
  • Read steps (Health Connect): to show your steps, as described above.
  • Notifications and run at startup: to deliver the reminders you set.
  • Camera: Waypoint asks your camera app to take a meal or progress photo; it does not access the camera directly.

Deleting your data

Use More → Erase everything in the app, or uninstall Waypoint, to delete all of your data from the device. Revoke step access in Health Connect at any time. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Waypoint is meant for adults and is not directed at children.
